## Demo accounts

The seed creates 23 fictional staff. When the database is first created, the terminal prints each person's name, role and PIN. Nothing else, in the application or in this repository, shows a PIN.

| Role | People in the seed | Try |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | 1 | Everything, including Owner intelligence, HR, accounting and permissions |
| General manager | 1 | Everything except managing permissions |
| Restaurant manager | 1 | Reservations, floor, orders, staff, inventory, analytics; no profitability view |
| Host | 1 | Reservations, table assignment, seating |
| Waiter | 10 | Waiter app: tables, order pad, courses, bill |
| Bartender | 2 | Waiter app: orders and payment |
| Kitchen | 5 | Menu and availability |
| Cashier | 1 | Taking and refunding payments |
| Inventory manager | 1 | Warehouse |

To print the PINs again, stop the demo, delete `sufra-os/nova.db` (and `nova.db-wal`, `nova.db-shm`) and start it again. This discards anything you changed.

## A ten-minute tour

1. **SUFRA 360** — enter the restaurant, pick a date and time, show tables, reserve one. Note the booking code.
2. **Administration → Reservations** — find the booking; it is waiting for the restaurant's decision. Accept it.
3. **Administration → Floor** — seat a party at a table. Return to SUFRA 360 and watch that table's lantern change.
4. **Waiter app** — sign in as a waiter, open a table, add items by seat, send a course.
5. **Administration → Kitchen** — move the ticket through its states.
6. **Administration → Warehouse** — open *Dish ingredient usage* to see the recipe quantities posted by that ticket.
7. **Administration → Analytics** and **Owner intelligence** — forecasts and risks computed from the seeded history.
8. Switch language with **EN / AZ / RU** at any point.

## What the demo data is

- Everything is generated by `sufra-os/app/seed.py` and `seed_ops.py`: 30 tables, 23 staff, about 40 dishes, 60 ingredients, 100 guests, about 150 reservations and 180 days of trading history.
- Names are invented. Seeded bookings carry no email addresses or phone numbers.
- The restaurant, "SUFRA Waterfront", and its address are fictional. Weather is the real current weather for Baku.

## Safety

- The seeded PINs follow a predictable pattern and are four digits. They are for a database on your own machine. **Do not expose a seeded database to the internet without resetting every PIN** (Administration → Settings → Reset employee PIN).
- `NOVA_SEED_DEMO=1` only has an effect when no database file exists. It never overwrites one.
- The demo reset endpoint is disabled unless `NOVA_ALLOW_DEMO_RESET=1`.
- The database, logs, the staff-badge signing key and uploaded photos are git-ignored.
- There is no authentication bypass or backdoor account.
