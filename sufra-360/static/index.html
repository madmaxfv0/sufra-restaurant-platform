    Sufra Waterfront

    A sample venue built for the SUFRA Immersive Reservations prototype. Nineteen tables across a garden room, a main dining room, the bar, a private room and a waterfront terrace.

    About what you're looking at

    Every table, seat, sightline and 360 view in this prototype is marked PLACEHOLDER. The layout is illustrative. It is generated, not measured, and it is not a photograph of a real room. The software is built so that measured floor plans, LiDAR scans, photogrammetry and real 360 captures drop into venue/ and replace the placeholders without any change to the booking logic.

    Verification levels

    • PLACEHOLDER — generated approximation. What you see now.
    • MEASURED — from architectural plans and tape measurements.
    • SCAN_VERIFIED — from LiDAR or photogrammetry.
    • PHOTOGRAPH_VERIFIED — a real 360 capture from that seat.
